# Impjieg Brand Strategy

## Brand Position

Impjieg is **not** a generic job board.  
Impjieg is Malta's hiring signal layer: clearer jobs, clearer employer intent, clearer salary signals, fresher listings, less hiring noise.

## Brand Promise

**Jobs with clearer signals.**

Every element of Impjieg should communicate that this marketplace gives candidates and employers better information to make faster, more confident hiring decisions.

## Brand Personality

| Trait | Expression |
|---|---|
| Sharp | Clean layout, fast interactions, no bloat |
| Trustworthy | Verified employers, salary transparency, legal compliance |
| Practical | Useful features, not gimmicks. Salary optional but encouraged. |
| Local | Malta-first, but not tourist-coded. Professional Maltese tech community. |
| Modern | 2026-grade SaaS UX. Not 2015 job board templates. |
| Signal-driven | Data visualised as signals, not noise. Freshness indicators, trust badges. |
| Calm but confident | Blue/teal foundation. No screaming orange buttons. No casino neon. |

## What We Are NOT

- Not a government employment registry
- Not LinkedIn-style social network
- Not an AI resume-scanner wrapper
- Not a generic "job alert" aggregator
- Not a casino-adjacent platform
- Not a cheap template marketplace

## Visual Keywords

Signal, clarity, motion, trust, focus, local relevance, clean data, hiring momentum.

## Voice and Tone

* **Direct**: Say what the product does. No "revolutionising the hiring landscape."
* **Useful**: Every sentence should help someone act.
* **Low-fluff**: If it sounds like marketing filler, cut it.
* **Professional**: But not cold. Warm enough for humans.
* **Candidate-friendly**: Respect job seekers' time and privacy.
* **Employer-trustworthy**: Clear pricing, clear signal, clear compliance.

## Brand Rules

1. Never claim fake job numbers.
2. Never imply every job has salary if salary is optional.
3. Never use fake metrics.
4. Never overpromise AI/matching if not implemented.
5. Prefer "signals" over "magic."
6. Prefer clarity over hype.
7. Salary transparency is a feature, not a mandate. Show it when it exists. Say "not disclosed" when it doesn't.
8. Freshness matters. Show timestamps. Expire old listings automatically.
9. Malta is the primary market but the design should work globally.
10. No dark patterns. Marketing consent is opt-in, never bundled.

## Tagline

**Current**: Jobs with clearer signals.  
**Verdict**: Keep. It is distinctive, accurate, and already deployed.

Alternative taglines (not replacing):
- Malta's hiring signal
- Hiring, with clearer signals  
- Clearer signals for Malta hiring

## Conversion Strategy

1. **Candidate**: Browse → See salary/work-mode signals → Apply directly → No middlemen.
2. **Employer**: Post a role → Add salary for better visibility → Receive direct applications → Manage pipeline.
3. **Trust loop**: Transparency → Better matches → Better outcomes → More trust.
