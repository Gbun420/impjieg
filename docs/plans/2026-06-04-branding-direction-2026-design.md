# Impjieg 2026 Branding Direction

## Goal
Refresh Impjieg into a 2026-ready brand that feels:

- trustworthy
- editorial
- human
- clear
- locally grounded in Malta

## Research Summary

The strongest 2026 brand signals are consistent across current design research:

- Bold typography and stronger hierarchy are back. Figma notes that typography is moving beyond legibility into storytelling, while The Branding Journal and Wacom both point to expressive type, bold minimalism, and stronger contrast.
- Motion is now part of brand identity, not an afterthought. TDS DaaS highlights motion-first identity systems as a 2026 baseline.
- Audiences are moving away from generic minimalism and toward tactile, human, imperfect, and authentic visuals. Canva, Creative Bloq, and Stills all point to this shift.
- Trust matters more in hiring than visual hype. Indeed, Mercer, and Employ all reinforce that job seekers want salary clarity, transparent process cues, and fewer trust gaps.

## Recommended Direction

Use a "transparent editorial" brand system.

This is the best fit for Impjieg because the product's value proposition is already trust-based: salary clarity, verified employers, fresh listings, and direct applications. A loud, playful identity would fight the product. A sterile SaaS identity would undersell it.

## Brand Positioning

### Short statement
Malta's transparent job board.

### Brand promise
Every job should be easy to judge quickly: what it is, what it pays, and whether it is worth applying for.

### Tone

- direct, not fluffy
- calm, not cold
- confident, not salesy
- local, not parochial

## Visual System

### Color

- Background: warm limestone off-white
- Foreground: deep ink navy
- Primary: marine blue
- Secondary: sea-glass green
- Accent: clay coral

This keeps the palette reduced and high-contrast, which matches 2026 direction while avoiding the purple SaaS default.

### Typography

- Display: Fraunces
- Body: Manrope

Use the display face for headlines and key brand moments. Keep body copy clean and highly readable.

### Layout

- editorial spacing
- clear content hierarchy
- card-based modules with restrained borders
- no cluttered gradients or oversized decoration

### Motion

- light fade-in and subtle rise
- no excessive motion or decorative movement
- motion should support comprehension, not compete with it

### Logo treatment

- use a compact icon + wordmark lockup
- keep the mark simple and reusable
- allow the full wordmark to live in the header and footer

## Messaging Direction

Recommended homepage framing:

- "Jobs in Malta, with pay up front."
- "Transparent hiring for Malta."
- "No ghost jobs, no hidden pay."

Recommended value pillars:

- salary clarity
- fresh listings
- direct applications

## What To Avoid

- purple generic SaaS palettes
- over-animated hero sections
- vague aspirational copy
- heavy glassmorphism
- cluttered multi-color systems
