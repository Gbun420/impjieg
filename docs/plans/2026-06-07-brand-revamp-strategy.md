# Impjieg 2026 Brand & Design Revamp Strategy

## Executive Vision
Transform Impjieg from a static job board into a **sentient, human-centric career ecosystem** for Malta's tech, digital, and iGaming talent. The 2026 identity will move away from "corporate blue minimalism" toward **"Nature Distilled"** aesthetics—combining organic textures with invisible, agentic AI technology.

---

## 1. Visual Identity: "Nature Distilled"
As a reaction to hyper-polished AI imagery, the 2026 look for Impjieg will emphasize **authenticity and tactile depth**.

- **Color Palette:** 
  - **Base:** Earthy, muted tones (e.g., "Cloud Dancer" whites, warm stone grays).
  - **Accents:** "Dopamine Saturated" pops of color (Neon Mint for success, Coral for urgency) to guide the eye.
- **Typography:** 
  - **Kinetic Architecture:** Edge-to-edge viewport-scaled headings (vw) that react to scroll and interaction.
  - **Serif Contrast:** Pairing a bold, brutalist sans-serif for utility with a high-contrast serif for editorial career stories.
- **Imagery:** 
  - **Employee-Generated Content (EGC):** Replace stock photos with raw, "TikTok-style" video snippets and "imperfect" behind-the-scenes office shots.
  - **Tactile Textures:** Subtle film grain and paper-like finishes on UI cards to signal human craft.

## 2. Sentient UX: The "Invisible Engine"
Move away from traditional forms and dashboards toward **agentic interactions**.

- **Generative UI:** The candidate dashboard will no longer be a fixed grid. It will be a **modular system** assembled on-the-fly based on the user's current goal (e.g., "Applying," "Learning," "Negotiating").
- **Conversational Search:** Replace the search bar with a **natural language agent**. Candidates should be able to say: *"I'm looking for a hybrid tech lead role in a sustainable startup with a learning budget"* and see a tailored, ranked list.
- **Zero-Friction Summaries:** AI-generated 3-point bullet summaries for every job listing, extracting key "Malta signals" like visa support, exact office location, and verified salary.

## 3. Sensory & Trust Branding
- **Sonic Identity:** Distinctive haptic feedback and a "success tone" for applications and verified offers to build emotional connection.
- **Privacy as a Feature:** Transparent "value exchange" indicators showing exactly how data is used to improve matching, reinforcing the "Malta hiring signal" of high trust.
- **Verified Competency:** Shift the narrative from "Resumes" to "Skill Badges"—AI-certified competency scores that are the primary visual anchor of a candidate's profile.

## 4. Implementation Roadmap
1.  **Phase 1: Brand System (Q3 2026):** Develop the kinetic logo and adaptive color system.
2.  **Phase 2: Agentic Search (Q4 2026):** Replace the legacy search filters with the natural language AI agent.
3.  **Phase 3: Generative Dashboards (Q1 2027):** Launch the modular candidate and employer workspaces.

---
*Strategy compiled on June 7, 2026 based on global design and branding audits.*
